Information you share

The contact form asks for a parent or guardian name, phone number, email, child's age group, enquiry type and message. Please do not include medical records or other sensitive documents.

How we use enquiries

Enquiries are emailed to kidsplanet674@gmail.com so the school can respond about admissions, daycare and visits. The website does not provide online payments or an online admission account.
