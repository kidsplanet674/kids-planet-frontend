Welcome to Kids Planet

Small discoveries.
Happy beginnings.

Looking for a play school in Old Town, Bhubaneswar? Kids Planet brings early learning and daycare together in a caring, colourful environment for children aged 2-6.

Our Preschool, Nursery, LKG and UKG classes make room for play, creativity, conversation and growing independence. With daycare from 8:30 am to 7:30 pm, families can plan care around their day.

  • Play-way learning with caring educators
  • AC rooms, CCTV and child-friendly play spaces
  • Music, dance, celebrations and creative activities

Our purpose

Our Mission

To provide a caring, play-based early learning environment where children aged 2-6 develop confidence, creativity, independence and social skills, while supporting families through preschool education and daycare.

Looking ahead

Our Vision

To help every child begin school life with joy, a sense of belonging and a lasting curiosity to learn.

From our principal

A happy beginning to a lifetime of learning

Mrs. Ranjita Patra
Principal, Kids Planet Play School & Day Care

Dear Parents,

Every child deserves a happy beginning?a place where they feel safe, valued and excited to explore. At Kids Planet, we believe that early learning grows through play, curiosity and the encouragement of caring teachers.

From Preschool and Nursery to LKG and UKG, our aim is to help children build confidence, express themselves and develop everyday skills at their own pace. Our daycare extends that care beyond school hours, supporting children and their families through these important early years.

We value the trust you place in us and believe that parents and teachers work best as partners. I warmly invite you to visit Kids Planet, meet our team and discover a welcoming place for your child to learn and grow.
