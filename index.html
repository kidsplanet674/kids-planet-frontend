What ages can join Kids Planet?

Preschool / Playgroup is for ages 2-3, Nursery for 3-4, LKG for 4-5 and UKG for 5-6. Contact the school to discuss the right class for your child.

What are the school and daycare timings?

School runs from 9:30 am to 12:30 pm. Daycare is available from 8:30 am to 7:30 pm. Please ask the team about operating days and holiday arrangements.

Is daycare available for all classes?

Yes. Daycare supports children in all four age groups, from Preschool / Playgroup through UKG (ages 2-6).

Where is Kids Planet located?

Plot No-1897, Badhei Banka Chowk, Old Town, Bhubaneswar, Odisha 751002. You can call us on 9337164626 for help with directions.

How can I enquire about admission or fees?

Message us on WhatsApp, call 9337164626 or use the contact form. Share your child's age and whether you need school, daycare or both. The team will explain availability, fees and the admission process.

What facilities are available?

Facilities include AC rooms, CCTV cameras, audio/video smart classes, an open play area, play equipment, dance and music, ball pool and sand pit activities. Transport is available; please enquire about routes and availability.
